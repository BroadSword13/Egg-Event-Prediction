Rules & model

Active scheduling rules and model settings are explicit, visible, and editable where useful.

Exclusivity rules

⛔
Daily event pools
Non-Ultra Hab Sale, Vehicle Sale, and Generous Drones cannot occur together. Non-Ultra Boost Time+, Generous Gifts, Shell Sale, and Mission Fuel Boost share the same Tuesday–Thursday pool and each conflicts with those three events. The equivalent Ultra event types share the Ultra pool and follow the every-other-day cadence described below. No additional same-day exclusions among Boost Time+, Generous Gifts, Shell Sale, and Mission Fuel Boost are assumed without evidence.
⛔
Same-type tier conflict
Ultra + Non-Ultra versions of the same event type cannot occur together.
✅
Cross-type combinations allowed
Example: Ultra Hab Sale + Non-Ultra Generous Drones can occur together, as confirmed Sep 10, 2026.

Prediction constraints

↗
Soft Non-Ultra gap estimates
All Non-Ultra rotations use their own gap history. Longer-than-usual waits increase an event’s chance without forcing a deadline. Missing history and unseen gaps retain a chance; weekday, minimum-gap, and same-day conflict rules still apply.
↔️
Ultra every-other-day cadence
From May 4, 2026 forward, modeled Ultra events are only eligible every other Pacific event day. Ultra Mission Capacity Boost must also land on one of those cadence days while retaining its Sunday-only rule. Same-rotation gaps from this era that do not align to the 2-day cadence are ignored by gap training.
🎂
Ignore July 14 (Egg Day)
All July 14 events are excluded from gap training, current-gap resets, and event modeling because the anniversary uses special events.

Recency weights

Rolling age bands are measured from the forecast/model reference date, not by calendar year. Weights affect gap frequencies used by the prediction engine; raw counts stay unchanged.

How probability is calculated

For a current gap g, the model asks: among historical gaps that lasted at least this long, what share ended exactly at g? That is the raw conditional hazard. Non-Ultra forecasts smooth this estimate to allow unseen gaps and extend it beyond the longest recorded wait.

P(hit at g | survived to g) = weighted count(g) ÷ weighted count(gaps ≥ g)

A Monte Carlo simulation then moves day by day, resets rotations after simulated hits, applies the Ultra every-other-day cadence, and resolves same-day conflicts across all modeled event rotations.
