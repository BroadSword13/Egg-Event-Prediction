Rules & model

Active scheduling rules and model settings are explicit, visible, and editable where useful.

Exclusivity rules

⛔
Daily event pools
Non-Ultra housing, shipping, and drones cannot occur together. Non-Ultra 2× boost duration, 2× gifts, 15% off shells, and 3× fueling share the same Tuesday–Thursday pool and each conflicts with those three events. The equivalent Ultra event types share the Ultra pool and follow the every-other-day cadence described below. No additional same-day exclusions among boost duration, gifts, shells, and fueling are assumed without evidence.
⛔
Same-type tier conflict
Ultra + Non-Ultra versions of the same event type cannot occur together.
✅
Cross-type combinations allowed
Example: Ultra housing + Non-Ultra drones can occur together, as confirmed Sep 10, 2026.

Prediction constraints

↔️
Ultra every-other-day cadence
From May 4, 2026 forward, modeled Ultra events are only eligible every other Pacific event day. Ultra 2× Capacity must also land on one of those cadence days while retaining its Sunday-only rule. Same-rotation gaps from this era that do not align to the 2-day cadence are ignored by gap training.
🎂
Ignore July 14 (Egg Day)
All July 14 events are excluded from gap training, current-gap resets, and event modeling because the anniversary uses special events.
The raw Non-Ultra housing history contains at least one gap longer than 16 days. The 16-day cap is therefore treated as a configured prediction rule, not a claim that every historical gap obeyed it.

Recency weights

Rolling age bands are measured from the forecast/model reference date, not by calendar year. Weights affect gap frequencies used by the prediction engine; raw counts stay unchanged.

How probability is calculated

For a current gap g, the model asks: among historical gaps that lasted at least this long, what share ended exactly at g? That is the conditional hazard.

P(hit at g | survived to g) = weighted count(g) ÷ weighted count(gaps ≥ g)

A Monte Carlo simulation then moves day by day, resets rotations after simulated hits, applies the Ultra every-other-day cadence, and resolves same-day conflicts across all modeled event rotations.
